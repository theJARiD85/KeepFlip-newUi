Request account and data deletion

This page is for KeepFlip: Reseller Assistant. You can request deletion here even if you cannot sign in to the app.

Email a deletion request

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address on your KeepFlip account, or include that address in your message so we can find the account.
  2. Ask us to delete your KeepFlip account and associated data. You may include your KeepFlip account ID if you have it. Do not send your password.
  3. We will verify the request, process the deletion, and contact you to confirm the result. We may ask for information needed to verify that you own the account.

We delete the account and associated data when the verified request is completed. Limited information may be retained where required for legal compliance, security, or fraud prevention. See our Privacy Policy for details.

If the email button does not open an email app, send your request to support@keep-flip.com with the subject “KeepFlip account and data deletion request.”
