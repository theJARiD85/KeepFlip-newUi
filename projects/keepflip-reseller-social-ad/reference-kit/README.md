# KeepFlip transparent reference kit

These assets deliberately isolate KeepFlip's visual language instead of supplying a full app screenshot. They are intended for AI-video references, compositing, and motion treatment.

- `keepflip-icon-transparent.png`: actual KeepFlip icon with its white backdrop removed.
- `keepflip-scanner-target.png`: static version of the scanner target treatment from `ValueRadarTargetGraphic`.
- `keepflip-evidence-field.png`: transparent evidence-network and valuation-range motion element derived from `ValuationEvidenceField`.
- `keepflip-analysis-terminal-chrome.png`: transparent-boundary terminal card chrome derived from `AnalysisTerminal`, with no readable text.

Use one or two assets at a time. Do not give a generative video model full phone screenshots and these assets together unless the phone must appear in the shot.

Suggested instruction to put beside the uploads:

> Treat the supplied transparent KeepFlip PNGs as small visual motifs and motion overlays. Integrate or reinterpret them around the phone or object; never show an uploaded PNG as an unchanged flat image, screen insert, or full-frame card. Do not recreate a full app screenshot unless explicitly requested.
