## Business summary

### Customer problem

People who buy or sell secondhand items often lack the time and specialized knowledge to determine exactly what an item is, whether it is worth repairing, how quickly it may sell, and whether the likely resale value justifies the cost and effort. That uncertainty leads to missed resale opportunities, weak pricing decisions, and time-consuming manual research across multiple services.

### KeepFlip solution

KeepFlip brings the discovery and decision workflow into one mobile experience. Users can photograph an item, save it to inventory, and receive guided information about identity, condition, resale value, market evidence, repair effort, likely resale velocity, and next actions. The product is designed to distinguish between high-confidence information and situations where additional identifying photos or market evidence are needed.

### Customers

- Independent resellers and side-hustle sellers
- Thrift, estate-sale, garage-sale, and flea-market shoppers
- Collectors and hobbyists
- Households selling unwanted or inherited items
