Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer you are looking for? Reach out to us here.

What is Hi3D?

Hi3D, developed by Math Magic, is a 3D generation tool powered by our proprietary high-resolution AI model Sparc3D. Simply input a single reference image to instantly generate studio-ready 3D assets with industry-leading quality, democratizing 3D creation. This breakthrough solution empowers game developers, designers, and 3D artists to efficiently unleash creativity and accelerate creative workflows from concept to deployment.

What are the key advantages of Hi3D?

Hi3D 's key advantages lie in generating models with outstanding geometric structures and high engraving precision that can be directly used for printing.

What 3D file formats does Hi3D support?

Hi3D supports downloading models in multiple formats, including .glb, .obj, and .stl (for 3D printing), which are compatible with mainstream 3D modeling software (such as Blender, Unity and Maya) and 3D printing software.

Are the 3D models generated on Hi3D private?

Yes. By default, all models you generate are private and visible only to you. You can generate a share link on the model details page to share them publicly.

Why do subscription credits reset at the end of the billing period?

Hi3D subscription credits are issued on a monthly (or annual) basis and represent your usage quota for the current billing period. When the billing period ends, these credits will automatically reset to zero, and the system will issue new credits for the next period. The system will remind you before your credits expire, so please use them within the validity period to avoid waste.

What can Hi3D do?

The 3D models generated on Hi3D can be used in various scenarios, including game development, visual effects production, product modeling, and 3D printing.

How to use Hi3D?

On Hi3D , all it takes to generate a 3D model is uploading a single image. Supported formats include .JPG, .JPEG, .PNG, and .WebP, with a maximum file size of 20MB.

Is Hi3D safe and legal?

Hi3D adopts industry-standard security measures to protect user data, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all uploaded content. For details, please review our Privacy Policy.
